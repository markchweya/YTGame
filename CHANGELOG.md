# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Realistic highway

- Perspective-mapped asphalt, gravel shoulders and grass; painted lines; guardrails.
- Roadside props: lamp posts, trees, signs, billboards, overpasses, finish gantry.
- Hills and curves, dusk sky, layered mountains, distance haze.
- Pre-rendered car sprites with three body styles (Viper GT, Brawler V8, Phantom X).
- Off-road driving with drag, shake and dust; guardrail bounce.
- New HUD: ticked speedometer with needle, gear, nitro segments, start-light gantry.
- New garage menu with live car showcase, body-style stats and paint picker.

## 0.1.0 — First playable

- Pseudo-3D road, five AI rivals, race and endless modes.
- Obstacles, pickups, combo scoring, local top-10 leaderboard.
- Procedural audio, touch controls.
